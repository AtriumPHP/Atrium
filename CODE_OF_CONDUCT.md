# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
Atrium community a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless
of background or identity.

## Our standards

We expect all participants to:

- Be considerate, collaborative, and respectful in language and actions.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, derogatory comments, sustained
disruption, and any conduct that a reasonable person would consider
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1. The full text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>, and the
community-impact enforcement guidelines apply.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at
**marcin.j120@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and
security of the reporter of any incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the
project's leadership.
